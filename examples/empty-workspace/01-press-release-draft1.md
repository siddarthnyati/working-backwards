# A workspace that starts as a team

## New workspaces now begin with teammates in the room and something real to work on — and inviting never blocks anyone.

**¶1** — **SAN FRANCISCO, 12 MARCH 2027** — Today we are rebuilding the first five minutes.
Creating a workspace now starts with your team: an invite step in first-run setup, an
invite composer available from anywhere, and empty states that offer real starting points
instead of a blank page. Every step is skippable — a solo workspace is a choice we respect,
not a failure we nag about.

**¶2** — Most workspaces die alone. 61% never gain a second member in their first two weeks
`[OBSERVED: product analytics]`. Teams that don't invite churn because they never experience
collaboration — the product's entire value. The invite screen is buried three clicks deep in
settings `[OBSERVED]`, and new users tell support the workspace "felt empty" `[REPORTED:
CS onboarding verbatims]`.

**¶3** — From today, setup asks who you work with — right after your workspace gets a name.
Type addresses or skip in one click. The composer is available from every screen, and the
full invite funnel is measured: attempted, sent, accepted, first-action.

**¶4** — Today a team lead who wants company either digs the invite screen out of settings —
which analytics says almost nobody finds in week one `[OBSERVED: <4% of new workspaces open
it]` — or shares raw document links over email, which creates guests with no workspace and
no way back. The first is invisible. The second is unmeasured. Both look, from our side,
like a user who chose to be alone.

**¶5** — The blank page is gone too. A new workspace opens on a template gallery — a
project tracker, a team wiki, a meeting-notes space — each one pre-populated and each one
usable in one click. An empty state that only says "create your first document" is an
instruction; one that offers a working starting point is a head start.

**¶6** — Customer quote:

> "I set it up on a Tuesday, meant to invite the team, couldn't find where, and by Friday
> we were back in the old tool. It wasn't a decision. It just never started."
> — team lead, 12-person agency
> `[illustrative construction — not a real customer statement]`

**¶7** — Spokesperson quote:

> "We kept optimizing what a user sees on day one and ignored who they see — nobody. The
> single strongest pattern in our retention data is teams versus solos, so the first thing
> setup should do is help you not be solo."
> — VP Product

**¶8** — **Getting started.** New workspaces get the new flow automatically. Existing
workspaces get the composer and template gallery from the sidebar.
