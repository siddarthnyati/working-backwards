A workspace that starts as a team

New workspaces begin with teammates in the room and something real to work on — and inviting never blocks anyone.

Why now

61%of new workspaces never gain a second member in 14 days[OBSERVED: product analytics, 6-mo cohort]
5.4×day-30 churn, single-member vs 3+-member workspaces[OBSERVED: retention cohorts]
<4%of new workspaces ever find the invite screen in week one[OBSERVED: IA analytics]
?how many users try to invite and abandon — attempts are not instrumented[NEEDS EVIDENCE → R1]

The causal link — that teammates cause retention rather than merely accompany it — is [ASSUMED — C5], and this ships with the holdback experiment that tests it (REQ-D3).

Who it affects

The team lead who meant to invite everyone

team leads at 5–50-person companies creating a workspace self-serve, intending to bring their team

Today: sets the workspace up on a Tuesday, can't find the invite screen (it's three clicks into settings), works alone in an empty canvas, and is back in the old tool by Friday. Not a decision — it just never started.

After

setup asks who they work with; the workspace opens on real content

Invites are step 2 of setup — or one click to skip, with no nagging ever. The first screen is a template gallery, not a blank page. Every invite shows its status: sent, opened, joined, bounced.

The experience

Setup · step 2 of 3
"Who do you work with?" — email chips, Send invites & continue or Skip for now (one click, no dialog). Import-from-contacts is present but disabled: "pending privacy review."
First open
Template gallery — project tracker, team wiki, meeting notes, each usable in one click. "Start empty" is always the last card.
Composer, anywhere
Sidebar invite composer with per-invite status. A bounce shows "bounced — check address" with a resend, in the composer, same day.
Failure state
If the send fails server-side, it says so and keeps the typed addresses. Silence never renders as success — the current defect, not rebuilt inside the fix.

What it deliberately does not do

No contact import until privacy, regulatory and security review answer (BLK-01 · 02 · 06 — the disabled button is the honest version). No invite incentives (BLK-05). No re-prompting anyone who skipped — the never-nag rule is a requirement (REQ-DP5), not a promise.
