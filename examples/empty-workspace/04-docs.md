# Inviting your team — documentation draft

Drafted from the approved PR and external FAQ. One section could not be written.

## Setting up a workspace

Step 2 of setup asks who you work with. Type email addresses, or select **Skip for now** —
you can invite from the sidebar at any time. Nothing in your workspace is locked behind
inviting anyone. Source: PR ¶1 / PR ¶3.

## Starting from a template

A new workspace opens on the template gallery. Each card creates a working page — a project
tracker with example tasks, a team wiki, meeting notes. **Start empty** is always available
as the last card. Templates create content; they never overwrite anything. Source: PR ¶5.

## Invite states

| State | Means |
|---|---|
| Sent | The email went out |
| Opened | The invite link was opened |
| Joined | They're in — their name appears in members |
| Bounced | The address didn't accept mail — check it and resend |

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | What to do |
|---|---|
| Invite shows "bounced" | Check the address; resend from the composer |
| Invitee says "no email" | Ask them to check spam; resend generates a fresh link |
| Wrong person invited | Revoke from Members before they join; the link dies immediately |
