# The trial that ends politely

## Nobody gets charged by surprise: you'll know before we bill you, and cancelling takes one tap.

**¶1** — **AUSTIN, 3 APRIL 2027** — Today we're ending the surprise. Before a trial
converts to a paid plan, we say so — by email and in the app — and cancelling takes one
tap, no retention maze. Receipts now show the next renewal date, every time.

**¶2** — This is for one specific person: the trial user who stopped opening the app by
day 3 and gets charged on day 14 anyway — the forgot-cohort. They are 71% of our eventual
refunders `[OBSERVED: activity × billing join]`. 38% of last quarter's refunds cite
"forgot to cancel" `[OBSERVED: refund log]`, "surprise charge" is the #1 support category
in week three of every cohort `[OBSERVED: ticket tags]`, and their reviews say "tricked"
`[REPORTED: review verbatims]`. Whether those reviews cost us conversions is something we
believe and have not measured `[ASSUMED — C6]`.

**¶3** — Two days before the first charge, we tell you — an email and an in-app banner —
with two buttons: keep it, or cancel in one tap.

**¶4** — Today the renewal date lives in account settings, where nobody who has stopped
opening the app will ever see it. The current "fix" is the refund queue: the customer
discovers the charge on a bank statement, gets angry, writes the review, then asks for
their money back. We refund the money. The review stays.

**¶5** — Cancelling is one tap from the reminder, the banner, or the receipt — never more
steps than subscribing took. Receipts state the next renewal date and price.

**¶6** — > "I didn't even remember signing up. The charge showed up and I felt scammed —
which is stupid, because I liked the app." — trial user, consumer plan
> `[illustrative construction — not a real customer statement]`

**¶7** — > "Every one of these charges is revenue this quarter and a detractor forever.
We'd rather earn the conversion than ambush it. And because finance believes reminders
will cost us conversions and I believe they won't, this ships with the experiment that
settles it." — VP Product

**¶8** — **Getting started.** Nothing to enable. Reminders start with the next trial cohort.
