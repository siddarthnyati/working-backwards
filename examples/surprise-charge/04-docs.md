# Your trial, your renewal — documentation draft

## What happens at the end of a trial

Two days before your first charge we send one reminder email and show a banner in the app,
with the price and date. You can keep the plan or cancel in one tap from either. Source: PR ¶3.

## Cancelling

One tap from the reminder, the banner, or any receipt. Never more steps than signing up
took. Your data stays for 30 days in case you come back. Source: PR ¶5.

## Receipts

Every receipt shows the amount, the next renewal date, and the next price. Source: PR ¶5.
