## Context tier

**Tier:** `1 · attached`

**Files read:**
- `wb/context/2026-platform-strategy.md` — 2 pages. Owner: VP Platform. Reviewed January
  2026. Three pillars, a stated investment ratio, an explicit not-doing list, and a section
  headed "known gaps in this document."

**What this makes evaluable:**

- **Dimension 4 (strategic fit)** — the critic can now cite and quote rather than ask. It
  still does not rule: citing a pillar is not the same as knowing whether the VP would fund
  this quarter, and the document itself says the ratio is "where the arguments will happen."
- **IFAQ-03 (what are we not doing instead)** — answerable, and the answer is uncomfortable.
- **IFAQ-16 / launch framing** — the document's "honest limits stated up front" language is
  a usable constraint on how the launch communication is written.

**What this does *not* make evaluable, and it is most of it:**

- **Dimension 6 (falsifiability)** — no latency data, no prior post-mortems. Q-18, Q-19 and
  Q-20 are unchanged from the tier-0 run and two of them still have no owner. A strategy doc
  is the wrong instrument for these.
- **BLK-01 through BLK-04** — security, privacy, legal and regulatory. A strategy document
  has nothing to say about erasure obligations or SOC 2 controls. These would need DPAs,
  the control narrative, and counsel. Six blockers is six blockers.
- **BLK-05 (ownership)** — the strategy doc raises the gap explicitly under "known gaps": *no
  ownership map for the integration layer; platform and partnerships have overlapping
  claims.* Naming a gap is not closing it. The blocker stands, and it now has a citation
  behind it, which makes it harder to dismiss.
- **Stage 5 baselines** — no metric definitions were supplied. The document even says pillar
  2 is unmeasured.

**Honest summary:** one document moved four questions and zero blockers. That is the real
exchange rate, and it is worth stating plainly rather than implying that context resolves
risk. What it resolves is *your own* uncertainty about your own company. The rest was never
in a document.
