The mechanics.

Every agent in the pipeline, the exact prompt that drives it, the judge that reviews it, and the gate that follows. One honest note first: this is nine hats on one model — each "agent" is the same model re-invoked with a different prompt, a different reference file, and a different template. The behavior difference is engineered entirely in the prompts, which is why they're all on this page.

Four beats, nine times.

1 · You prompt

One stage at a time. The prompt names the stage, the rules, and ends "do not continue."

2 · Author writes

The stage's reference file loads as its behavior spec; the template constrains shape; provenance tags carry.

3 · LLM as judge

A separate pass against the rubric. Six dimensions verdict; two only ever route questions to named humans.

4 · You gate

Accept, send back, or override — logged to DECISIONS.md, never argued. Then the next stage unlocks.

Inside the intake agent's head

Intake is deliberately the hard stage — problem formation is the whole game. It opens with one unled question: "What's on your mind? Say it however it comes out." Your answer is recorded verbatim, and the distance between that and the final problem statement is the stage's proof of work. From there, every answer runs through five scans:

#ScanFires whenWhat you hear
1Costume scanbuild / add / need / should in the problem statement "That's a solution wearing a problem costume — what did you observe?"
2Enumerable-customer"everyone," or a role with no segment "Could you name ten? What fields would the query filter on?"
3Number hygieneany figure without a source "Where does that live? If nowhere, it's [ASSUMED] — out loud."
4Causality scan"because / so that / which means" joining two observations "The correlation is observed. The because is its own claim."
5Inversion"nothing" to "what would make this not worth fixing?" "No kill condition means a belief, not a problem."
THE BUDGET — WHY THE PRESSURE ENDS Six challenges at intake, two at every later gate, counted out loud ("challenge 3 of 6"). At the limit, the agent stops: the unresolved challenge is written to DECISIONS.md with both positions and to QUESTIONS.md owned by you, and the pipeline proceeds without comment. Same philosophy as overrides — the record is the mechanism, the guilt is not. A tool that interrogates forever gets abandoned; a tool that never pushes is a form.

The interrogation closes with the one question that is never challenged: "What are you afraid the answer is — what do you believe here that you've never checked?" Those answers become Declared assumptions, and Stage 6 flags any requirement resting on them. Full protocol: references/interrogation.md.

Authoritative on six dimensions. Barred from two.

The single most important design decision. An LLM can verify a claim is made, specific, and sourced. It cannot verify the claim is true, the market is big enough, or engineering can build it — and a judge that returns confident verdicts on unknowable things simulates a review meeting without the knowledge that made review meetings valuable.

#DimensionClassOutputFails when
1Customer specificitystructuralPASS / REVISE / BLOCK "Everyone," or a bare role with no segment — could you build a list of ten?
2Evidence presencestructuralPASS / REVISE / BLOCK Significance asserted with nothing [OBSERVED] or [REPORTED] behind it — the most common real failure
3Alternative namedstructuralPASS / REVISE "There is no current solution" — there is always a current behaviour
4Strategic fitsubstantivea question + an owner role — never a verdict — (can observe a rationale is absent; cannot judge if a stated one is right)
5TraceabilitystructuralPASS / REVISE An artifact that doesn't cite the artifact above it; a requirement with no source
6Falsifiabilitysubstantivethe must-be-true list, as questions with owners — (feasibility claims route to engineering; asserting them is decoration)
7ClaritystructuralPASS / REVISE Jargon, passive voice hiding an actor, internal terms unexplained
8Template completenessstructuralPASS / REVISE Sections missing or stubbed. [NEEDS EVIDENCE] and [UNKNOWN] are complete; "TBD" is not
THE JUDGE'S OWN DRIFT GUARDS — from critic-rubric.md Two failure modes it is told to watch in itself: softening ("could benefit from additional supporting data" is a PASS in a REVISE costume — if it fails, say it fails and give the sentence to change) and drifting substantive ("this seems strategically sound" is the single thing that makes the tool untrustworthy — convert it to a question). On REVISE it must name the sentence and what it must establish; on BLOCK, which earlier stage to return to.

No documents? The interview builds you a context pack.

Most context isn't in a document — it's in the user's head. At tier 0 the skill offers a ten-minute interview: six slots (strategy, metrics, what exists, ownership, constraints, evidence), written to wb/context/elicited.md with honest provenance. Offered once, never required.

The rules that keep it honest

Every answer is [REPORTED: the user], never [OBSERVED] — testimony isn't a document. Hedged answers ("I think…") are written as [ASSUMED], out loud. The best question in the interview: "what do you believe about this problem that you have never actually checked?" — those answers become Declared assumptions, and Stage 6 flags any requirement resting on them.

WHAT IT BUYS — dimension 4 cites your own stated priority (marked self-reported); blocker owners become real names. WHAT IT NEVER BUYS — closed blockers. Testimony addresses legal and regulatory questions better; it answers none of them.

Resume is the difference between a skill and a long prompt.

Resume the working-backwards session in wb/<session-id>/. Read session.json,
tell me the current stage, the open blockers, and what the critic said last.
Then continue.

session.json holds mode, current stage, every verdict, every override, and the open blocker and question arrays — updated after each stage. Days later, the session picks up exactly where the last gate left it.
