Your trial, your renewal

What happens at the end of a free trial, and how to cancel in one tap.

What happens at the end of a trial

Two days before your first charge we send one reminder email and show a banner in the app, with the price and the date. You can keep the plan or cancel in one tap from either. That's the whole program — one email, no follow-ups.

Cancelling

One tap from the reminder, the banner, or any receipt. Cancelling never takes more steps than signing up did. Your data stays for 30 days in case you come back.

Receipts

Every receipt shows the amount you paid, the next renewal date, and the next price — plus a cancel link. You should never have to go looking for when you'll be charged next.

Not yet documented — on purpose

"What if I was already charged and didn't mean to be?"
The answer for past charges is a legal and commercial decision that hasn't been made (→ BLK-03, owner: legal counsel). Until it lands, support handles refunds case by case, and this page stays silent rather than promising. A help page that can't be written yet is a policy that hasn't been decided — recording the gap is the point.
